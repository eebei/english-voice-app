Meet the Founder

Why I built PITWALL

Yuji
Founder, OMORAY PITWALL
Ex-kart racer · 8 yrs running a race circuit · 8 yrs in motorsport import · lifelong F1 obsessive

I have lived close to racing for much of my life.

I raced karts.
I worked for a kart importer.
I later worked as a circuit manager.

Racing was never just about cars for me. It was the tension before a start, the sound of engines, the pressure of a braking point, the frustration of a mistake, and the joy of finding one better lap.

In 2014, while working as a circuit manager, I built a racing simulator with my own money and placed it at the kart track. At the time, that was still unusual. We used it for driver coaching, time attack events, customer experiences, and even brought it to events for Toyota dealer sponsors and TOYOTA GAZOO Racing.

The simulator worked. Drivers learned faster. People competed. They smiled. And I felt it clearly: racing simulators had a future.

Later, I found iRacing. It brought back the feeling of racing. The focus before a corner. The tension of wheel-to-wheel driving. The frustration of a mistake. The joy of discovering a better line, even years later. I still find new ways to drive. Even now, during PITWALL testing, I can find a new way through a chicane and feel that small racing joy again.

People often ask me if I love cars. The truth is simpler.
I love racing.

But sim racing has one quiet problem.
Most drivers experience it alone.

You sit in your room. You hold the wheel. You push. You make a mistake. You get frustrated. You improve, little by little. And when the session ends, you are left alone in the room, soaked in sweat.

In real racing, a driver is never truly alone. There is an engineer. A strategist. A team. A voice on the radio. That voice gives information, strategy, calm, and direction. It helps the driver reset after a mistake. It turns the race into something shared.

I wanted to bring that feeling to sim racers.

So I started building OMORAY PITWALL. An AI Race Engineer for iRacing. A voice in your headset. A partner before the race, during the race, and after the checkered flag.

Not just telemetry.
Not just analysis.
A presence beside you.

“Racing without this AI engineer feels lonely now… this is a must.” — early tester

That is why I build PITWALL.

For sim racers everywhere. For the kids and grown-ups who dream of racing. For every driver chasing one better lap alone in a room. May your races never feel lonely again.
