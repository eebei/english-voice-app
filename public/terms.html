1. The service

OMORAY PITWALL ("PITWALL") is an AI race-engineer application for iRacing. It provides in-race voice communication, strategy and telemetry-based information, and post-session debriefs. PITWALL requires a separate, active iRacing subscription and a Windows PC that meets iRacing's own requirements (Windows 11, 64-bit; Windows 10 reached end-of-life in October 2025). If your PC runs iRacing, it runs PITWALL. PITWALL is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by iRacing.com Motorsport Simulations, LLC.

2. Starter Pass, billing & included use

  • The current PITWALL offering is the Starter Pass: US$9.99 (tax-exclusive where applicable), paid once through Stripe.
  • It is not a subscription; there is no free trial and no automatic renewal.
  • The Pass is available for 30 days from activation and includes a defined usage allowance for live race engineering, practice, and debrief. The app shows remaining included use.
  • “About five race sessions” is a usage guide, not a guarantee. Heavier or longer use can consume the allowance sooner; when either the 30-day period or included allowance ends, access ends.

3. Cancellation & refunds

  • Because Starter Pass is a one-time purchase, there is no subscription to cancel and no future automatic charge.
  • Access ends when the 30-day period or included usage allowance ends, whichever occurs first. As a digital service delivered immediately, payments already made are generally non-refundable.
  • Nothing here limits rights you may have under mandatory consumer-protection law in your country.
