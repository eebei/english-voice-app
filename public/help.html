How do I download and install PITWALL?

After you subscribe, your welcome email has a download link — or grab it anytime from your welcome page. It's a single Windows app: just run the .exe, nothing else to install. Then see Setup for push-to-talk and choosing your engineer.

I paid but can't log in

Open the app and choose member login, then enter the same email you used at checkout. We email you a login link — open it on the same PC (check Spam / Promotions if it's slow). Access codes are only for invited testers; paying members log in by email.

"Account doesn't match" / access denied

The email in the app must match your subscription email exactly. If you checked out with a different address, log in with that one. Still stuck? Email omoraypitwall@gmail.com and we'll sort it fast.

It won't start until I update

That's the built-in update gate — we've shipped a newer build. Click the purple download button, run the new .exe, and you're current. Your login carries over.

Bridge

Windows blocked the .exe ("unknown publisher")

Normal for an indie app. Click More info → Run anyway. The Bridge only reads iRacing memory and talks to your own browser — nothing leaves your PC except your voice chat.

The black window closed instantly

Run it again and read the top line. If it mentions a missing module, re-download a fresh copy from Setup. If antivirus quarantined it, allow the file.

My engineer says "no telemetry / bridge not connected"

Three things must all be true:

1 — The Bridge window is open and says Waiting for iRacing... or iRacing CONNECTED.
2 — iRacing is running and you're on track (menus don't send data).
3 — The status bar at the top of the app shows 🟢 LIVE.

If the bar is 🟡, iRacing isn't on track yet. If 🔴, the Bridge isn't running.

Lap times work but no positions / traffic / strategy

Those only fire in race sessions — in practice there are no positions to call. Jump into a race and they wake up.

It resets every time I pit

Make sure you're on the latest Bridge — older builds reset on brief pauses. Update with the command below, then restart it.

App & Voice

The microphone keeps missing what I say

Use Google Chrome — its speech recognition is the most accurate. Speak after your engineer finishes; the mic re-opens automatically.

My engineer forgot our last session

Memory is stored in that browser. Use the same normal Chrome window on the same PC every time — Incognito keeps no memory. Cross-device memory arrives with accounts.

The page looks old / a feature is missing

Hard-refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R, or open a fresh tab.
