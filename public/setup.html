Setup Guide

Up and running in 5 minutes

Windows + iRacing. One app, no coding required.

1

Download OMORAY PITWALL

One file — your race engineer, the voice pipeline, and the iRacing telemetry reader are all bundled in. Nothing else to install.

⬇ Download OMORAY-PITWALL-Desktop-latest.exe
Windows may show "unknown publisher." That's normal for an indie app — click More info → Run anyway.
2

Log in with your Founding Season email

On first launch, click "アクセスコードをお持ちでない方はこちら（会員ログイン）" and enter the same email address you used to subscribe. We'll send a login link.

Open that email, click the link — it shows a short code. Copy it and paste it back into the app, then press ログイン.

No password, no separate ID to remember. Your subscription email is your login.
3

Pick your engineer & set your push-to-talk button

Choose from James, Hajime, 官兵衛, or 大石. Then assign a free button on your wheel or keyboard for push-to-talk — hold it to speak, release to listen.

4

Start iRacing

Launch iRacing and get into a session as usual. OMORAY PITWALL connects automatically the moment you're on track — no alt-tabbing, works behind fullscreen.

✓

You're on the wall

A status indicator shows 🟢 LIVE when telemetry is flowing. Now just drive — your engineer will call the data and wait for your reply.
