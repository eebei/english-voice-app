Setup Guide

Up and running in 5 minutes

Windows + iRacing. No coding required.

1

Download the Bridge

The Bridge is a tiny app that reads iRacing's telemetry and passes it to your engineer. Python is bundled in — nothing else to install.

⬇ Download OMORAY-PITWALL-Bridge.exe
Windows may show "unknown publisher." That's normal for an indie app — click More info → Run anyway.
2

Run the Bridge

Double-click the file. A small black window opens and shows:

OMORAY PITWALL Bridge BUILD ... started Waiting for iRacing...

Leave this window open while you drive. That's it — the Bridge is live.

3

Start iRacing

Launch iRacing and get into a session as usual. The Bridge connects automatically the moment you're on track.

4

Open the app & pick your engineer

In Chrome, open the app, enter your name, and choose your race engineer. Pick a mode, tap the mic to go LIVE, and you're racing with a crew.

▶ Open the app
Use a normal Chrome window (not Incognito) so your engineer remembers you between sessions.
✓

You're on the wall

A status bar at the top shows 🟢 LIVE when telemetry is flowing. Now just drive — your engineer will call the data and wait for your reply.
